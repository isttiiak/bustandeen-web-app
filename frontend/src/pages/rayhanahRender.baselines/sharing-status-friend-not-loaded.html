What is istiḥāḍa?

Istiḥāḍa (irregular/non-menstrual bleeding) is bleeding that goes beyond the maximum period length (Ḥanafī: 10 days, majority: 15 days), or that occurs outside the normal menstrual pattern.

The Prophet ﷺ told Fāṭimah bint Abī Ḥubaysh: "That is a vein, not menstruation. When your period comes, stop praying, and when it ends, wash the blood off and pray." (Bukhārī 306)

During istiḥāḍa:

  • Ṣalāt resumes: perform wuḍū for each prayer time
  • Fasting is valid: no makeup needed for these days
  • Intimacy is permitted (majority view)
  • Quran recitation is permitted

Rayhanah automatically flags when your cycle exceeds the maximum for your chosen madhab. If you're unsure, consult a scholar you trust.
