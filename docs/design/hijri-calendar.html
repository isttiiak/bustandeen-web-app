/library/calendar. Real dates for Bangladesh. Tap a day, the settings icon (top right) and Download. The print preview under the phones follows the phone's download choices. Both phones share one state.

Layout: Corners. The calendar you put first in the download sheet is the big number, the second is top right, the third sits under the big number one size smaller. The lower part of each day box holds the day's events (colour coded) or stays blank for writing by hand.

My suggestions in this version

  • Several events on one day (PDF): show all of them in the day box, colour coded, each on its own short line in priority order: Islamic first, then government holiday, then national day. A box has room for two lines; a third becomes "+1 more" and the "This month" column on the right always lists everything. One colour per kind, the same on screen and on paper: sage green = Islamic, red = government holiday, gold = national day, brown = weekend. Black and white uses line styles instead (double, solid, dotted) and underlines holidays.
  • "Inside each day" choice in the download sheet: "Events" (default) or "Blank for writing" (light ruled lines; the right column still lists the events).
  • Bengali calendar: off by default and only switched on in Calendar settings (third by default). It is written in Bangla script, digits and month names, in both languages, so it never looks like the other two.
  • Weekend: chosen in Calendar settings (default from your country: Friday and Saturday for Bangladesh), shaded brown in the app and on paper.
  • National days (gold) are days the country marks that are not holidays (Armed Forces Day 21 Nov, Martyred Intellectuals' Day 14 Dec). Curated with the holiday list, Bangladesh first.
  • Next 30 days lists yearly Islamic days and country days only; Fridays, Monday/Thursday fasts and Ayyām al-Bīḍ stay on the grid and in "This month".
  • Download range: this month, a full year, or a range of months ("From" and "To" in the main calendar). After "Download PDF", a short notice explains the dates (Maghrib, expected months, holiday source) with Cancel or "Open print sheet"; that text is no longer printed.
  • Still open: the app shows 11 and 12 Oct as "30 Rabīʿ al-Ākhir" (wavy underline in October). Fix proposed as a separate small PR.
