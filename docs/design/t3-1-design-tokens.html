Bustandeen design tokens: three directions

Three ways to give the app its own look, all built on the current warm olive, sage and gold palette. Each direction is shown on Home, Salat and Zikr, in dark and light. These are rough sketches with real tokens, not finished screens. Pick one direction (or mix parts) and T3.2 turns it into tailwind.config.js and migrates screens one PR at a time.

2 radiiOne for cards, one for controls. Today the app uses five (xl, 2xl, 3xl, lg, full).
3 elevationsFlat (border only), raised (cards), overlay (sheets, dialogs). No glows.
1 accent per screenGold marks the one thing that matters on a screen. A gradient only on its hero.
Ornament as structureDividers, completion marks and milestones. Never wallpaper.
Branded light themeWarm paper instead of DaisyUI's stock white, for reading outdoors.
ContrastEvery text pair below is AA (4.5:1 or more); muted text no longer uses white/30.

What I need from you

  1. Which direction: A, B, C, or a mix (for example A's frames with B's radii).
  2. Light theme paper: parchment, linen or sage paper.
