T4.3 Morning and evening adhkar

One screen: /library/adhkar becomes a guided routine. One adhkar at a time, a big tap counter, auto-advance when the count is reached. Home's card shows progress. Tap the counter to try it. Both phones share the same state.

Proposed set (verified on sunnah.com, 2026-10-10)

# Adhkar Count When Source, grade as shown on sunnah.com
1 Ayat al-Kursi 1 both Quran 2:255 (Tanzil text)
2 Ikhlas, Falaq, Nas 3 each both Tirmidhi 3575, Hasan (Darussalam); Tirmidhi: hasan sahih gharib
3 Asbahna / Amsayna wa asbaha al-mulku lillah (full) 1 both Muslim 2723b, Sahih
4 Allahumma bika asbahna / amsayna 1 both Tirmidhi 3391, Sahih (Darussalam); Tirmidhi: hasan
5 Sayyid al-Istighfar (full) 1 both Bukhari 6306, Sahih
6 Bismillahilladhi la yadurru 3 both Abu Dawud 5088, Sahih (al-Albani); Tirmidhi 3388
7 A'udhu bi-kalimatillahit-tammat 1 evening Muslim 2709a (2708b), Sahih
8 Subhanallahi wa bihamdihi 100 both Muslim 2692, Sahih
  • Found while verifying: the live "Raditu billahi Rabba" item cites Abu Dawud 5072 as Hasan, but sunnah.com grades 5072 Da'if (al-Albani), and its wording is "radina ... wa bi-Muhammadin rasula" with no count of 3. The "raditu ... nabiyya" wording is Tirmidhi 3389 (Tirmidhi: hasan gharib, no count). Not in the routine above; needs your decision.
  • Progress: per-item counts on this device for the tracking day; "morning done" / "evening done" synced per day and shown on Home. Nothing is added to zikr totals, Noor or streaks.
  • Counts can be tapped past by "Next", so a partly done routine is fine. Done = every item reached its count.
  • "Show all" keeps today's full list view for people who read along without counting.
  • Bangla meanings are drafts for your review.
