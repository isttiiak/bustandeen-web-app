T3.4 E: quick habit sections, prototype

Home at 375px, dark and light, below the arch. Frozen at Thursday 8:10 PM in Dhaka. Habit order (from onboarding) = Salat, Quran, Zikr, Fasting; all sections on.

Static mock for review only, not app code. Numbers are examples. The gold numbered dots mark the open decisions listed under the phones.

Decided (Istiak, 2026-10-09)
  1. Salat: the timeline is the Salat section and stays optional (D); the arch row always stays.
  2. Today's goals stay, below the quick sections.
  3. Quran: "Continue" goes to the khatam position when khatam mode is on, otherwise to the last thing read anywhere. Below it, "Listen" and "Pick a surah".
  4. Zikr: three chips chosen in Zikr settings (default Astaghfirullah 100, Lā ilāha illallāh 100, Ṣalawāt 100). The tap action is also a Zikr setting: open the counter on that dhikr with its number as the target (default), or add the number in one tap.
  5. Fasting: "Fasting today?" with one "Yes" (a second tap undoes it). While fasting, a "Broke" button appears next to it. Plus the sunnah heads-up for tomorrow. "Excused" on Rayhanah days.
  6. Settings → Home screen: the four sections in habit order, on/off + up/down.
  7. Size: full cards.
